Paleoclimate Modeling · Climate Dynamics

Jinzhe Zhang

PhD Student, Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences

I study the global climate system from a paleoclimate perspective, focusing on climate model simulations, the hydrological cycle, cryosphere change, and multiscale variability from orbital to millennial timescales.

Current Focus

Connecting paleoclimate simulations with glacier behavior, monsoon variability, and future climate insight.

Research Interests

Climate Through Deep Time

Paleoclimate Modeling

Coupled climate model simulations for past climate states, including interglacial and glacial periods.

Hydrological Cycle

Global and regional monsoon change, moisture transport, and precipitation mechanisms under different boundary conditions.

Cryosphere

Glacier-climate interactions across High Mountain Asia, with attention to equilibrium line altitude and climate sensitivity.

Multiscale Variability

Climate changes from tectonic and orbital forcing to millennial variability and abrupt transitions.
